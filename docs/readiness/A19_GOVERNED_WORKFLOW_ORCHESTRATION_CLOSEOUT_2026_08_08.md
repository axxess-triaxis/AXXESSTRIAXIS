## Why This Document Exists

A-19's criterion was never a small thing to prove: "Timeline shows source, AI answer, human
decision, action, and audit event" -- five distinct elements, each backed by a different subsystem
(Knowledge Hub indexing, the RAG pipeline, the AI Review Inbox decision flow, the Tasks & Workflow
work-item repository, and the audit log), all required to trace back to **one single record**. This
program's own synthesis document (`HITL_LOOP_COMPLETION_KANBAN_2026_07_26.md`) explicitly held the
line on this for two weeks: "No single live pass has exercised the loop start-to-finish... cannot
move to `Yes` from code evidence alone." Every other closure in this program's history that touched
a piece of this loop (A-13, A-15, A-16, A-17, A-18) closed one link in the chain. This is the first
closure that closes the whole chain, observed together, live, in one sitting.

This is why the founder asked for it documented in detail rather than folded into the matrix row
alone: it is not just "one more actionable closed" -- it is direct, screenshotted proof that
AXXESS's core governed-AI-workflow proposition (an AI answer never silently becomes a real action;
it passes through human review, and the entire path leaves an audit trail) is not a design intent
or a partially-wired feature. It is a real, functioning, end-to-end system, in production, today.

### 3. AI Workspace -- the governed RAG answer itself

`https://landing.triaxisventures.com/ai-workspace`: a real query returned a governed answer.
