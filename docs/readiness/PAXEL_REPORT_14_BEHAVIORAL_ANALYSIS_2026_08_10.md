**Cross-checked against real tracker data, same session:** `docs/readiness/ACTIONABLES_READINESS_MATRIX.md`
(101 items, 70 `Yes`), the AXXESS Lite `XLA-*` tracker (26 items, 2 `Done`), and

`Resolved` -- itself already a direct, dated response to an earlier Paxel finding, see that
document's own "Why this exists" section) together show 195 tracked items, ~124 closed-equivalent,
with named root-cause grouping (e.g. the SMTP/DNS cross-cutting blocker collapsing 5 separately-
numbered defects into one dependency) already in active use.

**How this relates to this report's own 9/98 decision-closure finding, not overriding it:** these are
different things being measured. The 9/98 figure (this document's own "Decision patterns identified"
section above) is about *strategic/product decisions* recorded with a verified outcome; the
195/~124 figure here is about *tracked implementation defects/actionables* closed with evidence. Both
can be true at once -- a program can have strong defect-closure discipline (this correction) while
still lacking a habit of closing the loop on higher-level redirects and product calls (the original
finding, which is what motivated the Decision Ledger rule adopted above). Recorded as a real,
evidenced correction to the closure-read on defects specifically, not a rebuttal of the whole report.
