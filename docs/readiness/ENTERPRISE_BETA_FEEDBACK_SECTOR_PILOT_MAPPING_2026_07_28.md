## Purpose

This note maps the fresh enterprise beta survey's pilot-interest and budget signals to the respondent's stated sector, role/employment context and organization type.

It exists to support fundraising/application language without inflating survey interest into paying customers or signed pilots.

## Integrity Notes

- The ZIP contains 10 raw response PDFs.
- Responses 7 and 8 are an exact duplicate by respondent, organization, timestamp, score and answer values.
- Response 1 identifies as a core team member of the listed organization and should be separated from external-market claims where necessary.
- The survey contains personally identifiable information. This public repo note intentionally uses respondent numbers and role/sector context only, not emails or full raw respondent records.
- Pilot intent is survey-stated interest, not contracted traction.
