## 1. Sakura Law Chambers -- Strategic Collaboration and Referral Agreement

**Document type:** Engagement letter formalizing a non-exclusive strategic collaboration and
referral arrangement (titled "IN RE: Strategic Collaboration and Referral Agreement").

**Parties:**
- **Sakura Law Chambers** ("Sakura") -- signed by Shradhanjali Sarma, Partner, Startup &
  Corporate Advisory.
- **Triaxis Ventures Private Limited** ("Triaxis") -- signed by Sudipta Koushik Sarmah, Founder.

**Dates:**
- Letter dated: **19.06.2026**.
- Digitally signed by Sudipta Koushik Sarmah: **24-06-2026, 09:17 am**.
- Sakura's counter-signature (Shradhanjali Sarma) appears on the document as a signature block;
  no separate digital-signature timestamp is shown for that side in the source PDF.

**Purpose / scope:**
Non-exclusive collaboration to refer, introduce, and manage professional services between the
two firms, covering legal, fundraising, investor readiness, pitch deck, and allied advisory
services.
- **Sakura may refer to Triaxis:** clients requiring fundraising advisory, investor readiness,
  pitch deck preparation, investment support, and allied services.
- **Triaxis may refer to Sakura:** clients requiring legal, regulatory, corporate, commercial, or
  startup advisory services.

**Collaboration models (Section 1.1):**
1. **Referral Model** -- the introducing party hands off the client; the service-providing party
   independently contracts, invoices, and manages that client relationship (unless otherwise
   agreed in writing).
2. **Managed Delivery Model** -- one party stays client-facing (billing, communication,
   relationship management); the other delivers backend services to that client.

**Commercial terms (Section 2):**
- All commercial terms are agreed case-by-case, in writing (email counts).
- Referral Model: service provider invoices/collects directly; any referral incentive is
  separately agreed per engagement.
- Managed Delivery Model: the delivery party communicates its professional fee to the
  client-facing party; the client-facing party sets final client pricing and **may apply an
  administrative/coordination margin of up to 15% over the delivery party's professional fees**,
  unless a different margin is agreed in writing for a specific engagement.
- Each party is solely responsible for its own taxes, statutory filings, and invoicing.
- This letter is the **master framework** governing future collaborations unless otherwise
  agreed -- individual future engagements still need their own written scope/commercials/timeline.

**Relationship structure (Section 3):**
- Explicitly **not** a partnership, joint venture, agency, employment, or fiduciary relationship.
  Neither party can bind or represent the other without express written agreement.
- Mutual confidentiality obligation over client and commercial information.
- Explicitly non-exclusive both ways -- each party may work with competing firms/clients; a
  disclosure-and-good-faith-resolution process applies if a material conflict of interest arises
  on a specific proposed engagement.
- Client-facing party owns client communication/approvals; delivery party owns execution of the
  agreed scope.

**Termination (Section 4):**
- Either party may terminate with **30 days' prior written notice**.
- Termination does not affect engagements already accepted before the termination effective date.
- Parties cooperate in good faith on transition/handover for any engagement in progress at
  termination.
- Undisputed outstanding amounts are payable within **7 days** of the relevant invoice.

**What this document does not establish:** no specific client, deal, or fee amount is named --
this is a framework agreement; each actual referred engagement requires its own written
scope/commercial terms per Section 2.5.
